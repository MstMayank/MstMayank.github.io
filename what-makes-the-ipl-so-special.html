The IPL Never Really Ends, Does It?

By Mayank Shekhar Tiwari · 6 November 2026 · 3 min read

The number of IPL trade rumours I've come across in the last week is ridiculous. And I won't lie, I absolutely love this part of the IPL.

We haven't even reached the auction, let alone the actual tournament, and people are already fighting over team combinations.

Look at the discussions around CSK, Mumbai Indians and Punjab Kings. Every other day there's a new rumour, and with it comes another debate. Why would they trade him? Who's going to replace him? What exactly is the management thinking?

I've caught myself spending more time thinking about these possible trades than some actual matches.

And that's something I find quite fascinating about the IPL.

A few years ago, I barely cared about things like auction purses, retention strategies or squad balance. Now I find myself looking at a rumoured trade and wondering whether it actually solves a team's problem.

Because a good player isn't necessarily the right player for every team.

There's also this strange attachment we have to certain players. We want franchises to be practical with their decisions, but the moment they consider letting go of someone we love, all that practicality goes out of the window.

And I get it. Cricket isn't always about logic.

Half these rumours might never turn into actual deals. But look at how much they've already given us to talk about.

That's probably what I enjoy most about the IPL. Even when there's no cricket happening, there's always something happening around cricket.

The matches may last a few hours. The season may last two months. But for the fans, the IPL never really ends.
