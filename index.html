Fall of wickets

Five moments that got me. This is my innings with cricket.

  1. 1–2003

    The first match

    India v Australia, the World Cup final. That night, my love affair with cricket began.

  2. 2–2003

    Dravid

    My first favourite. Calm, patient, impossible to get out.

  3. 3–2005

    Dhoni

    Long hair, huge sixes, no fear. He felt like one of us.

  4. 4–Always

    Sachin

    Every elder had a story about him, and every story made the game bigger.

  5. 5–Now

    My generation

    Kohli the King, Rohit the Hitman, Gill the Prince and Bumrah, the bowling god.
