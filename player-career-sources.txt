Transformations:
Stable identifiers link source records. Name matching is unique and conservative.
Null/absent values mean unavailable, not zero. No career totals are inferred from partial match coverage.
First-class includes Tests; List A includes ODIs. IPL is not equivalent to all T20 cricket.
